If someone has shared intimate images or videos of you on echowire without your consent, we will remove them. This covers real images and recordings, deepfakes and other manipulated or AI-generated images, and threats to share them. Our [Community Guidelines](/guidelines) prohibit this (section 4.7), and we treat these reports as urgent.

## You do not need to send us the image

Please do not email us the image or video. A message link, channel, or username is enough for us to find it. If you can, keep your own copy of the evidence somewhere private before it is removed.

## How to report

**In echowire.** Open the message or the person's profile, choose Report, and pick "Intimate images shared without consent, including deepfakes, or a threat to share them". The report goes straight to our moderators.

**By email.** Write to <safety@echowire.org> and include:

- where the content is: a message link, the channel, or the username of the person who shared it
- that it is intimate content shared without consent
- whether you are the person shown or reporting on their behalf
- a way for us to contact you

If you are under 18, or the image shows someone who is, say so in your message. We handle that as a child safety report.

You can also ask a trusted friend, a lawyer, or a support organisation to report for you.

## What we do

When we receive a valid report we will:

- remove the content, aiming to do so within 24 hours and in any case within 48 hours
- block the exact file from being uploaded again, and look for other copies we can identify (an edited or re-saved copy may need to be reported separately)
- take action against the account that shared it, which can include suspension or a permanent ban
- keep a record of the report and the content where the law requires it, and report to law enforcement where required, which always applies to imagery of minors

We do not tell the person who shared the content who reported it.

## Other help

- [StopNCII.org](https://stopncii.org) is a free tool for adults. It creates a digital fingerprint of an image on your own device, so the image itself never leaves it, and participating platforms use the fingerprint to block the image.
- [Take It Down](https://takeitdown.ncmec.org) works the same way for images that were taken before you turned 18.
- The [Cyber Civil Rights Initiative](https://cybercivilrights.org) offers information and support for people affected by intimate image abuse.
- If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.

## If we got it wrong

If your own content was removed because of a report and you think that was a mistake, you can appeal at <appeals@echowire.org>.
